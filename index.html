Local practice

Practice, learner profiles, story problems, hints, worked explanations, adaptive practice and recommendations are free. Choose any combination of Find the answer, Find the missing number and Story problems in onboarding or Settings. In build 7, profiles, practice history and saved rounds stay on this device because iCloud learning-data sync is disabled. Restoring Pro in build 7 does not move that data to another device.

Upcoming build 10: your account, your choices

People keep their own Apple Accounts. Private iCloud sync will keep a person’s saved learners on their own account. Optional Shared learners invitations will let selected people view or edit one learner, including people outside an Apple family. Apple Family Sharing for eligible Pro access is separate: sharing a subscription never automatically shares learner data, and a learner invitation does not grant Pro.

Upcoming build 10: optional Practice Rewards

HartMath works on its own. Practice Rewards is an optional Pro connection to a separately configured compatible rewards service, off until a parent completes setup. It does not include a server or require one for learning. This connection is named BrightTurn in build 7. Read the setup guidance and privacy policy before enabling future-round summaries on a device.
