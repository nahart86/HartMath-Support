Local practice

Build 4 stores learner profiles and practice history on the device. Restoring Pro does not move that history to another device. iCloud learning-data sync also remains disabled in the current build 6 candidate.

Upcoming build 6: optional BrightTurn sharing

Build 6 will let parents with verified Pro access enable eligible future-round summaries for their own compatible BrightTurn server. This will require parent confirmation on each device; it will not enable iCloud sync or upload previous history. BrightTurn is not available in build 4. Read the privacy policy for the planned connection’s data handling.
