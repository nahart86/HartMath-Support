A space for every learner

Keep separate profiles and preferences, choose one operation or a mix, and save a round to continue later. Prefer a quick try? Start with Guest. Playful or standard sounds and optional Apple narration help practice feel comfortable.

Your account. Your choices.

Use your own Apple Account. Pro can sync your private learners across your devices, and you can separately invite selected people to one shared learner. Apple Family Sharing for eligible Pro purchases never shares learner data automatically.

HartMath works on its own. If you already use a compatible rewards service, the optional Practice Rewards connection can send eligible round summaries after you enable it on each device. A service is not included or required for learning.
