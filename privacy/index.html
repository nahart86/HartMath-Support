Privacy policy

Last updated October 10, 2026 · HartMath 1.2

HartMath is a math practice app for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. This policy describes the information handled by the current app.

Learner profiles and practice

HartMath saves learner names or nicknames, chosen avatars, practice preferences, optional profile PIN credentials, scores, rewards, practice dates and time, question attempts, and unfinished rounds on the device. You can use a nickname; a learner profile does not require an email address or a separate online account.

Saved practice history supports progress reports and continuing an unfinished round. Pro reports are calculated from that history. Guest rounds are not saved as learner history.

Profile PINs and parent controls

Current versions store a salted, derived PIN verifier rather than the entered PIN. Older local PIN records are upgraded when they are loaded. A profile PIN is a convenience lock for selecting a learner; it is not an online password or a guarantee that all device data is encrypted by HartMath.

On supported iPhone and iPad devices, parent controls can ask the system to authenticate the device owner. HartMath receives the authentication result, not biometric templates. A reading challenge is used when device authentication is unavailable and on Apple TV.

Device storage and iCloud

iCloud syncing is currently disabled. This version does not upload learner profiles, practice history or unfinished rounds to iCloud or a HartMath learning-data server. Selecting the same Apple Account on another device does not transfer this version's learner data.

Device backups are separate from HartMath syncing and follow your Apple device and backup settings. During storage recovery, HartMath may preserve damaged or interrupted local data on the device so that it is not silently replaced. Apple TV may remove local saved files to free space; local storage there is not a permanent backup.

If a later version enables iCloud learning-data syncing, this policy will describe the enabled behavior before that version is released.

Apple subscriptions

Apple processes purchases, billing and renewals. HartMath uses Apple's StoreKit service and verified transaction information to determine Pro access and restore an existing subscription. HartMath does not receive payment card details.

Subscription access and learner data are separate. Restoring Pro access does not transfer locally saved learner profiles or history. Deleting a learner or removing the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel through Apple's subscription controls. Apple's handling of App Store information is described in App Store & Privacy.

Voices, sounds and tracking

Question narration uses Apple's on-device speech synthesis with installed voices. HartMath does not record microphone audio or send question text to a separate narration provider. Sound effects are bundled with the app.

HartMath does not include advertising, third-party tracking analytics or advertising identifiers. Its educational reports describe practice within the app; they are not advertising analytics.

Your choices

You can change a learner's name, avatar and practice settings in Profiles. Delete a learner there to remove that learner from the current profiles and saved practice history on the device. Local recovery copies and operating-system backups are separate from the current learner list. Manage data on each device separately while syncing is disabled.

Parents and guardians can choose nicknames and control access to learner profiles. There is no need to send a child's real name or practice history when asking for general support.

Beta feedback and external pages

If you use TestFlight, Apple can share testing information and feedback with the developer, including crashes and usage information. See Apple's TestFlight information. This is separate from HartMath's educational progress reports.

These static information pages contain no analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub hosts this website and the public support tracker and handles service information under GitHub's privacy statement. Opening Apple or other links also contacts those services.

If an adult chooses to post a support issue, the developer and the public can read the GitHub username, message and any attachments. Use the tracker only for non-sensitive app questions. Do not post a child's name, profile PIN, learning history, Apple Account details or payment information.

Questions and updates

Parents and guardians can contact the developer through the support page for app or privacy questions. The linked issue tracker is public; ask a general question without including personal information. HartMath does not offer a private support form on this website.

Changes to this policy will be published with an updated date.
