Privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

HartMath provides math practice on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. This policy explains local learning data, optional Pro syncing and invitations, and the separate optional Practice Rewards connection.

Learner profiles and practice

HartMath saves learner names or nicknames, chosen avatars, practice preferences, optional profile PIN credentials, scores, rewards, practice dates and time, and question attempts on the device. Unfinished rounds are also saved on the device. You can use a nickname; a learner profile does not require an email address or a separate online account.

Saved practice history supports progress reports; Pro reports are calculated from that history. Saved state also supports continuing unfinished rounds. Guest rounds are not saved as learner history.

Profile PINs and parent controls

Current versions store a salted, derived PIN verifier rather than the entered PIN. Older local PIN records are upgraded when they are loaded. A profile PIN is a convenience lock for selecting a learner; it is not an online password or a guarantee that all device data is encrypted by HartMath.

On supported iPhone and iPad devices, parent controls can ask the system to authenticate the device owner. HartMath receives the authentication result, not biometric templates. A reading challenge is used when device authentication is unavailable and on Apple TV.

Device storage and iCloud

Some earlier versions kept learning data only on the device. Restoring Pro or selecting the same Apple Account alone does not transfer data from a version with syncing disabled. Update all devices before enabling sync or moving a learner into Shared learners, and keep the original device until its data has transferred.

Device backups are separate from HartMath syncing and follow your Apple device and backup settings. During storage recovery, HartMath may preserve damaged or interrupted local data on the device so that it is not silently replaced. Apple TV may remove local saved files to free space; local storage there is not a permanent backup.

Private iCloud learning data

When verified Pro access and syncing are active, HartMath uses Apple’s CloudKit service to sync saved private learners through the current iCloud account’s private database. This can include existing names or nicknames, optional last names, avatars, practice settings, derived PIN verifiers, scores, completed practice history, individual question attempts and unfinished rounds. Other devices using that same iCloud account can receive it. On Apple TV, this is the default user’s iCloud account, not the highlighted learner or another TV profile. Guest practice is excluded.

Private sync does not merge separate people’s Apple Accounts. Family Sharing for a subscription or iCloud+ storage does not grant another person access to these private records. Data is sent to Apple’s iCloud service rather than a developer-operated learning-data server. Practice Rewards credentials and queues are not part of this data.

Pausing syncing or losing Pro pauses automatic transfers; it does not delete retained local or iCloud data. Account changes stop the previous account’s work from being sent to the new account. Pending changes and recovery copies can remain on the device. Use one device at a time for an unfinished round and wait for syncing before switching; offline and concurrent work may not appear immediately elsewhere.

Explicitly invited Shared learners

A parent can select one saved learner and invite specific people through Apple’s private sharing interface on iPhone or iPad. People use their own iCloud accounts and can be outside an Apple family. Accepting a learner invitation does not grant Pro, reveal other private learners or connect a rewards service. Public learner links are not supported. Apple handles invitation delivery and share membership; the invitation identifies the learner and may display the owner’s name supplied by Apple.

The selected shared copy includes the learner’s name or nickname, optional last name, avatar, practice settings, scores, completed history and unfinished round. History can contain individual questions, submitted answers and learning progress. Existing history can be included when sharing begins. Profile PIN verifiers, other private learners, Practice Rewards credentials, pairing codes and queues, and Apple purchase records are excluded. The owner’s PIN is retained separately in private routing and recovery data; it is not given to invitees.

The owner chooses View only or Trusted editor. View-only participants cannot add practice or change settings. Trusted editors can change the learner’s shared data; this is broad editing access, not a restricted answer-submission role. Invite only trusted people. Reports and automatic shared transfers require the participant’s own verified Pro access. Apple Family Sharing can provide eligible Pro access independently, but never grants learner membership automatically.

Preparing the invitation moves that selected learner into Shared learners and retains a private recovery copy. Update HartMath on other devices first. Canceling the Apple invitation leaves the learner in Shared learners, accessible only to the owner until an invitation is accepted. A private routing record prevents older copies from sending that learner back as an ordinary private learner. Local migration, interrupted work and recovery copies are retained to avoid silently losing data; they do not all disappear when access is removed.

Accepted learners appear in a separate picker section. Direct viewing or practice does not authorize settings or data actions: those controls require a parent check, and the owner’s learner PIN still protects owner actions. Apple TV shows accepted shares for its default user’s iCloud account; invitations are created, accepted and managed on iPhone or iPad. Account changes, revocation or permission changes close editable access when detected. Offline devices cannot learn of every change immediately.

Parent-protected refresh, mobile JSON export and owner deletion remain available after Pro expires, using an online check of current account and permission. An editor’s export can include retained local work; a viewer’s export uses the current server snapshot. Exports contain that learner’s profile, settings, history, answers and saved round, with PINs and rewards credentials excluded. Share or save exported files only where you intend their contents to be accessible.

Stop sharing or Leave removes access, not the owner’s learner data. It cannot recall information already viewed or saved. Only the owner can confirm permanent deletion of a shared learner, with their existing PIN if set. The app first confirms a content-free deletion marker in iCloud, clears the private PIN binding, and then removes this device’s shared workspace and migration recovery copy. A content-free routing record remains to prevent stale copies from restoring the learner. Other devices remove their current shared workspace if they receive the deletion marker. Revoked or offline devices can instead retain local recovery copies; exported files and operating-system backups may also retain copies. A failed operation is reported rather than described as completed. Neither access removal nor learner deletion cancels Pro.

Optional rewards-service connection

Practice Rewards is optional. HartMath works without it. A parent with verified Pro can connect a saved learner to a separately configured compatible rewards server; it need not be family-operated. No rewards service is included. The connection stays off until a parent completes the check, enters the server’s HTTPS address and private pairing code, reviews the returned learner label and reward rules, and explicitly enables sending. HartMath does not create a service account or choose a server for you.

Sharing applies to eligible rounds started after enabling on this device. An unfinished round can also qualify when the learner explicitly continues it here, but only if sharing was already enabled on this device before that round originally started. Importing history or opening the app does not authorize an imported round for sharing. Each result contains a schema version, session identifier, completion time, number of distinct cards with submitted answers, number of those cards answered correctly, and active practice duration. HartMath does not send the learner’s name or local profile identifier, profile PIN, individual questions or answers, Apple Account details, or purchase records to the rewards server. Pairing does not upload completed history or rounds that began before enabling on this device. Guest practice cannot connect.

The pairing code is sent to the server you enter and is not retained as a saved code in HartMath. The resulting scoped connection credential, server address, returned learner label and rules, consent, pending results and receipt status are kept in a device-only Keychain item. The connection and pending queue stay on this device and do not transfer through private iCloud sync or Shared learners invitations. Each device needs its own parent-confirmed connection; this optional sharing does not enable learning-data sync. Pending results can remain locally while offline or while Pro is not verified; sending is attempted only while the app is open, online and Pro access is verified, and the connection and parent rule still permit it. Delivery is not guaranteed; the server may reject an expired result or connection.

Result sharing sends directly to the server you enter; it does not use a developer-operated central collection service. That server can associate results with the service’s learner selected during pairing and receives the ordinary connection information needed to handle an HTTPS request. Its operator controls its records, access, retention and deletion. Ask that operator about information already received. A local-network server may require the device’s local-network permission.

Disconnect remains available without Pro. It removes the connection and unsent results for that learner from this device and attempts to revoke the server credential. If the server is unreachable, revoke the connection using the service’s controls too. Successful revocation may cancel pending rewards if the service supports it. Disconnecting does not reverse rewards already granted; accepted server records follow the operator’s retention policy. HartMath history remains on the device. A reward depends on the parent’s server rules, limits and successful delivery; HartMath does not independently verify learning activity or guarantee a reward.

Apple subscriptions

Apple processes purchases, billing and renewals. HartMath uses Apple's StoreKit service and verified transaction information to determine Pro access and restore an existing subscription. HartMath does not receive payment card details.

Subscription access and learner data are separate. Apple may verify access for the purchaser or an eligible Family Sharing recipient. HartMath identifies recipient access and actual product eligibility when Apple supplies that information. Sharing Pro does not invite anyone to a learner or merge private iCloud data; the purchaser manages billing. Restoring access verifies Pro, while learning-data transfers follow the separate sync and invitation behavior described above. Deleting a learner or removing the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel through Apple's subscription controls. Apple's handling of App Store information is described in App Store & Privacy.

Voices, sounds and tracking

Question narration starts off for fresh settings and preserves an explicitly saved on/off choice. Narration uses Apple's on-device speech synthesis with available voices. HartMath does not record microphone audio or send question text to a separate narration provider. Sound effects are bundled with the app.

HartMath does not include advertising, third-party tracking analytics or advertising identifiers. Its educational reports describe practice within the app; they are not advertising analytics.

Your choices

You can change a private learner's name and avatar in Profiles, and practice preferences in Settings. For local-only data, deletion removes the learner from this device’s current profiles and saved history; manage each device separately while syncing is disabled. Private synced learner deletion can propagate to devices on the same account. Parent-protected Manage synced data export and deletion cover private synced learners only, excluding Shared learners, rewards-server records and local migration backups. Local recovery copies and operating-system backups are separate from the current learner list. Use the separate Shared learners controls described above for invited data. For the optional rewards connection, Disconnect removes local connection data; ask the service operator about records already received.

Parents and guardians can choose nicknames and control access to learner profiles. There is no need to send a child's real name or practice history when asking for general support.

Beta feedback and external pages

If you use TestFlight, Apple can share testing information and feedback with the developer, including crashes and usage information. See Apple's TestFlight information. This is separate from HartMath's educational progress reports.

These static information pages contain no analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub hosts this website and the public support tracker and handles service information under GitHub's privacy statement. Opening Apple or other links also contacts those services.

If an adult chooses to post a support issue, the developer and the public can read the GitHub username, message and any attachments. Use the tracker only for non-sensitive app questions. Do not post a child's name, profile PIN, learning history, Apple Account details or payment information.

Questions and updates

Parents and guardians can contact the developer through the support page for app or privacy questions. The linked issue tracker is public; ask a general question without including personal information. HartMath does not offer a private support form on this website.
