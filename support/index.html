For parents and teachers

Contact support

Ask a question or report a problem in the HartMath public support tracker. A GitHub account is needed to post. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support.

Open public support tracker

Posts and attachments are public. Do not include children's names, practice history, profile PINs, Apple Account passwords or payment details. Crop personal information from screenshots before posting. This is not a private support form.

What helps us investigate

  • Device model, system version and app version/build.
  • The steps you took, what you expected, and what happened.
  • The exact error message or a screenshot with personal details removed.

Beta testers can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight. See Apple's TestFlight feedback instructions.

Common questions

Start with the right learner

Complete the welcome flow on a fresh installation, or choose a learner from the full-screen profile selector. Add learner creates a separate profile. Guest is available for a quick round without saving learner history.

Use Profiles to edit a learner or manage an optional PIN. In Settings, “Ask who's practicing on launch” controls whether the selector appears the next time you open the app. On Home, use the profile button to switch learners.

Make practice yours

Settings lets you choose the operations, number range and cards per round. Number keypad and Multiple choice both work with Find the answer or Find the missing number. These choices apply to the next new round; a saved round keeps the settings with which it started.

Use Playful or Standard answer sounds and preview each outcome. For narration, turn on Read questions aloud, then choose Automatic or an installed Apple voice and a reading speed. The voices available depend on what is installed on the device.

Continue a saved round

A named learner can save an unfinished round and continue from Home on the same device. Choose that learner first, including its PIN if one is set. Guest practice does not create a saved learner round.

Cross-device learning sync is not enabled in the current version. Profiles, history and saved rounds remain on each device. Restoring a Pro subscription on another device does not copy that learning data.

Pro access and subscriptions

Free practice includes local profiles and immediate round results. An active HartMath Pro subscription unlocks saved reporting and analytics. The app displays the subscription price and billing period supplied by Apple.

If you already have Pro, open the Pro area from Progress or Settings, complete the parent check, and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that holds the subscription and allow time for Apple to verify it. If verification fails or no plans are available, keep the exact message for your support request.

You can manage or cancel a subscription through Apple's subscription controls. Deleting a profile or uninstalling the app does not cancel billing. Apple's standard terms of use apply to the app.

Navigation and readability

Home, Profiles, Progress and Settings are the main destinations. iPhone uses tabs. iPad provides a left sidebar when there is enough space and adapts when the window is smaller or text is larger. Apple TV uses a left-side menu; use the remote to move focus and Back or Done to leave a screen.

On Apple TV, Reveal answer asks for confirmation. Keep trying is selected first so you can cancel without revealing the answer. The app also adapts to supported larger text, reduced-motion and reduced-transparency settings.

Saved-data help

If a storage-recovery message appears, keep the exact wording and use the choices shown in the app. Do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step when you need to preserve local practice. A learner deletion is separate from subscription cancellation. Apple TV may remove local files to free space, so a copy stored only there is not a permanent backup.
