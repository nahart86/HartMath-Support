For parents and teachers

Contact support

Ask a question or report a problem in the HartMath public support tracker. A GitHub account is needed to post. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support.

Open public support tracker

Posts and attachments are public. Do not include children's names, practice history, profile PINs, Apple Account passwords or payment details. Crop personal information from screenshots before posting. This is not a private support form.

What helps us investigate

  • Device model, system version and app version/build.
  • The steps you took, what you expected, and what happened.
  • The exact error message or a screenshot with personal details removed.

Beta testers can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight. See Apple's TestFlight feedback instructions.

Common questions

Start with the right learner

Use Home’s learner button or Profiles to choose a learner. Profiles also lets you edit a learner and manage an optional PIN. Guest is available for a quick round without saving learner history.

The welcome flow helps create a learner and choose a starting level. “Ask who's practicing on launch” in Settings controls whether the learner selector appears when the app opens.

Make practice yours

Settings lets you choose operations, number range, cards per round and how to answer. In build 7, choose any combination of Find the answer, Find the missing number and Story problems in onboarding or Settings. Selected types have a checkmark; at least one must stay selected. In Settings, choose Done to close the selection sheet. New rounds mix only the selected types. Number keypad and Multiple choice work with each type.

Story problems, hints, worked explanations, adaptive practice and recommended practice are free. Adaptive practice starts off and can be enabled in Settings. Practice settings apply to the next new round; a saved round keeps its questions, answer choices and hint progress. Retry keeps the original questions but starts fresh answer choices and hints.

Use Playful or Standard answer sounds and preview each outcome. Narration starts off for fresh settings in build 7; an explicitly saved on/off choice is preserved. Turn on Read questions aloud, then choose Automatic or an available Apple voice and a reading speed. A saved voice keeps its name when available. If it is unavailable, Settings identifies that saved selection and narration uses an available fallback without discarding your choice. Voice availability depends on the device.

Continue a saved round

A named learner can save an unfinished round and choose Continue on Home on the same device. Choose that learner first, including its PIN if one is set. Guest practice does not create a saved learner round.

iCloud learning-data sync is disabled in build 7. Profiles, history and saved rounds remain on each device. Restoring a Pro subscription on another device does not copy that learning data.

Pro access and subscriptions

Practice, local profiles and immediate round results are free. HartMath Pro adds saved reporting and analytics. Where subscription plans are available, the app displays the price and billing period supplied by Apple. If plans are unavailable, local practice remains free.

Verified Pro also allows the optional rewards-service connection described below. In upcoming build 10, it also enables automatic private iCloud sync and transfers for explicitly invited Shared learners. Real Apple purchase, cancellation, Restore and Family Sharing recipient testing have not yet been qualified.

If you already have Pro, open the Pro area from Progress or Settings, complete the parent check, and choose Restore Purchases. Use the purchasing Apple Account, or an eligible Apple Family Sharing recipient account, and allow time for Apple to verify access. If verification fails or no plans are available, keep the exact message for your support request.

Upcoming build 10: The app will identify access received through Apple Family Sharing when Apple verifies it. Eligibility depends on the actual subscription and Apple’s sharing settings; the purchaser manages billing. Keep separate Apple Accounts. A learner invitation can include someone outside the Apple family, but that invitation does not supply Pro access. Neither Family Sharing nor a shared iCloud+ storage plan combines people’s private learner data.

You can manage or cancel a subscription through Apple's subscription controls. Deleting a profile or uninstalling the app does not cancel billing. Apple's standard terms of use apply to the app.

Upcoming build 10: private iCloud sync

Use the same iCloud account on your own devices for private learners. On Apple TV, iCloud uses the default user’s account; selecting a learner or another TV profile does not switch that account. With verified Pro and syncing active, existing saved learners, practice history and unfinished rounds can transfer through iCloud. Free local practice remains available without a subscription or server.

Check Settings → Sync & devices before moving an unfinished round to another device. Use one device at a time, leave the round, and wait for syncing to finish. Offline changes can wait locally. Restoring Pro verifies subscription access; it is separate from transferring learning data. Private data controls apply only to private synced learners, not to Shared learners or rewards-service records.

Upcoming build 10: invite people to one learner

On iPhone or iPad, select and unlock the saved learner, then open Settings → Sync & devices → Shared learners and complete the parent check. Guest cannot be shared. Choose View only or Trusted editor, read what will be shared, then prepare the invitation and choose specific people in Apple’s sharing interface. Public learner links are not supported.

Update HartMath on your other devices before moving a learner. Preparing an invitation moves that selected learner into Shared learners, including their existing history and saved round. If you cancel Apple’s invitation, the learner stays there and only you can access it until someone accepts. Other private learners remain private. Older app versions may need an update before account syncing can continue.

The recipient reviews the learner invitation and, when Apple supplies it, the owner’s name before accepting for their current iCloud account. Accepted learners appear separately in the learner picker: choose View for a view-only learner or Practice as for an editor. A parent check is not needed for each practice visit; shared settings and data controls stay locked until Parents & teachers: settings and data is opened and verified. An owner’s existing learner PIN still applies. Recipients do not receive that PIN.

View-only participants can open reports with Pro but cannot practice or change settings. Trusted editors can practice and change the selected learner’s shared data. Editing permission gives broad access to that learner’s data; invite only people you trust. Invited people need their own verified Pro access for automatic transfers and reports. Use one device at a time for an unfinished round; simultaneous editing and immediate handoff are not guaranteed.

On Apple TV, create, accept and manage invitations first on an iPhone or iPad using the same iCloud account as the TV’s default user. Refresh Shared learners on the TV to find accepted invitations. The highlighted TV profile does not select a different iCloud account.

In parent-protected management, Refresh shared learners and data controls checks current iCloud access even after Pro expires. Inside a learner’s parent-protected controls, iPhone and iPad can prepare a JSON export; Apple TV explains how to export on mobile. Only the owner can choose Delete shared learner permanently. Export and owner deletion do not require Pro, but do require an online account and permission check and the owner’s PIN if set.

Stop sharing and Leave are not deletion. Stop sharing removes invited access while keeping the owner’s learner. Leave removes the participant’s access without deleting the owner’s data. Owner deletion removes the selected learner and practice data from this device after server confirmation. Other devices stop showing it when they detect deletion or loss of access; some local recovery copies can remain. None of these actions can recall information someone already exported or saved. See the shared-data privacy details for recovery and backup limits.

Upcoming build 10: connect optional Practice Rewards

HartMath works without this connection. Practice Rewards requires a saved learner, verified Pro and a separately configured compatible rewards server. It does not include a rewards service, create a server account or make any HTTPS address compatible. The service need not be family-operated. Live reward delivery has not been qualified. Build 7 calls this connection BrightTurn; use that name in its Settings.

  1. In the compatible service, create a private pairing code for the intended learner and check its reward rule.
  2. In HartMath, select that saved learner. On iPhone/iPad open Settings → Parent connections → Practice Rewards; on Apple TV open Settings → About → Practice Rewards. Complete the parent check.
  3. Enter the full HTTPS server address without a page path, password or query, then enter the 24-character code. You can paste its grouped form with hyphens.
  4. Choose Check pairing code. Read the learner label, server and reward rules, then choose Enable sending future rounds only if they are correct. Cancel pairing if they are not.

Typing on Apple TV: A nearby iPhone or iPad can help enter the server address and pairing code. Use the same Apple Account as the Apple TV, turn on Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, then tap the keyboard notification on the iPhone or iPad when entering text on the TV. Follow Apple’s keyboard instructions for the requirements and steps.

Eligible rounds started after enabling on this device can be shared when finished. An unfinished round explicitly continued here can also qualify if sending was already enabled on this device before that round originally started. Importing history alone does not send it, and rounds that began before enabling remain excluded. The summary contains a schema version, session identifier, completion time, submitted-answer and correct-answer counts, and active practice duration. Showing an answer without submitting one does not count toward the answered-question target, and repeated guesses on the same card do not add extra answered cards.

The connection and waiting queue belong to this device and do not transfer through private iCloud sync or Shared learners invitations. Keep the app open and connected to attempt sending queued rounds; the connection and parent rule must still be valid. The status, Waiting to send count and receipt show progress; Try sending now retries when Pro is verified. Expired or unverified Pro pauses sending without stopping local practice. A received result does not guarantee a reward: the server applies its rules and daily limits. The connected service determines which day counts toward its limits and how old a result it will accept. Check that service’s rules; an offline result may arrive too late. HartMath does not independently verify learning activity.

If the connection storage cannot be opened, use Retry connection storage. If the server rejects an expired or revoked connection, disconnect and create a new pairing code. Do not post server credentials, pairing codes or a learner’s label in public support.

To stop sending, choose Disconnect and confirm. This remains available without Pro, removes unsent results from this device and attempts to revoke the server credential. Successful revocation may cancel pending rewards if the service supports it. Disconnecting does not reverse rewards already granted; accepted server records follow the service’s retention policy. If the app cannot reach the server, revoke the connection in the service’s controls too. This per-learner, per-device connection is separate from iCloud syncing and Shared learners.

Navigation and readability

The main destinations are Home, Profiles, Progress and Settings. iPhone uses tabs; iPad provides a left sidebar when space allows, adapting for smaller windows and larger text. Apple TV has a left-side menu.

On Apple TV, use the remote to move focus and Back or Done to leave a screen. Choose Customize on Home or move right from Settings in the sidebar to enter its controls.

On Apple TV, Reveal answer asks for confirmation. Keep trying is selected first so you can cancel without revealing the answer. The app also adapts to supported larger text, reduced-motion and reduced-transparency settings.

Saved-data help

If a storage-recovery message appears, keep the exact wording and use the choices shown in the app. Do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step when you need to preserve local practice. A learner deletion is separate from subscription cancellation. Apple TV may remove local files to free space, so a copy stored only there is not a permanent backup.
