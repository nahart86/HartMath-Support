A little help with practice

Choose a learner, make practice yours and pick up where you left off.

Get started · Pro · Sync · Shared learners · Contact

Start with the right learner

  1. Follow the welcome flow to create a learner and choose a starting level, or try Guest.
  2. Pick how you want to practice, then start a short round from Home.
  3. Use a hint when needed and review the round's results. A saved learner can continue an unfinished round later.

Use Home's learner button or Profiles to switch learners. Each saved learner keeps separate preferences and can have an optional PIN. “Ask who's practicing on launch” in Settings controls the opening selector. Guest practice does not save learner history or a round to continue.

Make practice yours

In Settings, choose addition, subtraction, multiplication or whole-number division, separately or in a mix. Set a starting level, number range, focus number and cards per round.

Choose any combination of Find the answer, Find the missing number and Story problems. Selected types have a checkmark; at least one stays selected. Choose Done to close the selection sheet. Number keypad and Multiple choice work with each type.

Story problems, visual hints, worked explanations, adaptive practice and recommended practice are free. Adaptive practice starts off and can adjust the challenge when enabled. New settings apply to the next new round; a saved round retains its questions, choices and hint progress. Retry uses the original questions with fresh choices and hints.

Choose the voice and sounds

Choose Playful or Standard sounds and preview the correct and try-again effects. Read questions aloud starts off; an explicitly saved choice is preserved. Turn it on to use Automatic or an available Apple voice, then choose a speed and preview it. If a saved voice is unavailable, HartMath identifies it and uses an available fallback without discarding the saved choice. Voice availability and quality depend on the device's installed voices.

Pro access and subscriptions

Practice, adaptive difficulty, stories, hints, profiles, local saved rounds and immediate results are free. An optional monthly or annual HartMath Pro subscription adds reports and analytics, private iCloud syncing and transfers for invited shared learners. Pro also enables the optional compatible-service connection below. The purchase screen shows Apple's current price and billing period before confirmation.

To restore existing access, open Pro from Progress or Settings, complete the parent check, and choose Restore Purchases. Use the purchasing Apple Account or an eligible Apple Family Sharing recipient account. Apple verifies access; restoring a purchase and transferring learner data are separate operations.

Family Sharing eligibility depends on the subscription and Apple's sharing settings. The app identifies shared access when Apple verifies it, and the purchaser manages billing. Keep separate Apple Accounts: a shared subscription or iCloud+ storage plan does not combine private learner data. An invitation to a learner does not grant Pro.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when confirmed. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel through Apple. Deleting a learner or uninstalling HartMath does not cancel billing. Apple's standard terms apply.

If plans or verification are unavailable, local practice remains free. Keep the exact message for a support request; never send account credentials or payment details.

Continue on your own devices

For private learners, use the same iCloud Apple Account on each device. Apple TV uses its default user's iCloud account; choosing a learner or another TV profile does not change it. Verified Pro and enabled syncing let saved learners, preferences, history and unfinished rounds transfer through private iCloud storage while the app is open. Guest rounds never sync.

Before switching devices, leave the round and use Save for later. Check Settings → Sync & devices and wait for syncing to finish, then open the receiving device and allow it to refresh. Use one device at a time for an unfinished round. Offline changes can wait locally, and immediate or simultaneous handoff is not guaranteed.

Pausing sync or losing Pro pauses automatic transfers; it does not delete existing data. Private export and deletion controls apply to private synced learners, not invited shared learners or a separate rewards service.

Using an older version? Some earlier releases kept learning data only on the device. Restoring Pro alone cannot transfer that data. Update HartMath on all devices before enabling sync or moving a learner into Shared learners, and preserve the original device until its data has transferred.

Invite someone to one learner

On iPhone or iPad, select and unlock the saved learner, then open Settings → Sync & devices → Shared learners and complete the parent check. Guest cannot be shared. Choose View only or Trusted editor, review what will be shared, then prepare the invitation and choose specific people in Apple's sharing interface. Public learner links are not supported.

Preparing an invitation moves that selected learner into Shared learners, including existing history and the saved round. If you cancel Apple's invitation, the learner remains there and is accessible only to you until an invitation is accepted. Other private learners remain private.

Recipients use their own iCloud accounts. After accepting, find the learner in the separate Shared learners picker section: View opens a viewer's workspace; Practice as opens an editor's workspace. Shared settings and data controls stay locked until Parents & teachers: settings and data is opened and verified. The owner's existing PIN still protects owner actions; recipients do not receive it.

View-only participants cannot practice or change settings. Trusted editors have broad access to change the selected learner's shared data. Invite only people you trust. Reports and automatic shared transfers require each participant's own verified Pro access. People outside an Apple family can be invited; subscription eligibility remains separate.

On Apple TV, create, accept and manage invitations first on an iPhone or iPad using the same iCloud account as the TV's default user. Refresh Shared learners on TV to find accepted invitations.

Parent-protected refresh, mobile JSON export and owner deletion remain available after Pro expires, with an online account and permission check. An owner's PIN is also required if set. Apple TV explains how to export on mobile. Exported files contain learning data; save or share them only where intended.

Stop sharing and Leave remove access, not the owner's learner. Only the owner can confirm Delete shared learner permanently. Deletion is applied after server confirmation; other devices react when they detect it. Offline or revoked devices can retain recovery copies, and exports or backups may remain. These actions cannot recall data someone already saved and do not cancel Pro. See the privacy details.

Optional Practice Rewards

HartMath works without a rewards server. This advanced Pro connection is for people who already have a separately configured compatible service. A service is not included, and an arbitrary HTTPS address is not enough.

  1. In the compatible service, create a private pairing code for the intended learner and check its rule.
  2. Select the saved learner in HartMath. On iPhone/iPad open Settings → Parent connections → Practice Rewards; on Apple TV open Settings → About → Practice Rewards. Complete the parent check.
  3. Enter the full HTTPS server address without a page path, password or query, then the private 24-character pairing code. A grouped code with hyphens can be pasted.
  4. Choose Check pairing code. Confirm the returned learner, server and rules before choosing Enable sending future rounds. Otherwise, cancel.

Setup, consent and waiting results stay on each device. Eligible rounds must originally start after sending was enabled on that device; an unfinished round explicitly continued there can qualify under that same original-start rule. Importing history never backfills results. Summaries contain a version, round identifier, completion time, distinct submitted-card counts and active duration, not learner names or individual questions and answers.

Keep the app open and online to send queued results, with Pro and the connection still valid. Waiting to send, receipt status and Try sending now show progress. Delivery does not guarantee a reward; the service applies its rules, limits and retention policy. HartMath does not independently verify learning activity.

Disconnect remains available without Pro. It removes this device's connection and unsent results and attempts server revocation. If the service is unreachable, revoke through its controls too. This does not reverse granted rewards or erase accepted server records; contact the service operator about those.

For TV typing, a nearby iPhone or iPad may offer Apple's keyboard notification when its account and connectivity requirements are met. See Apple's keyboard instructions.

Navigation and saved-data help

The main destinations are Home, Profiles, Progress and Settings. iPhone uses tabs, iPad adapts its sidebar for the available space, and Apple TV has a left menu. Use the TV remote to move focus and Back or Done to leave a screen. Customize on Home opens practice controls.

On Apple TV, Reveal answer asks for confirmation with Keep trying selected first. The app also adapts to supported larger text, reduced motion and reduced transparency settings.

If a storage-recovery message appears, keep its exact wording and use the offered choices. Do not uninstall the app as a first troubleshooting step if you need local work. Apple TV may remove local files to free space, so data stored only there is not a permanent backup.

Contact support

Ask a general app question or report a problem in the public support tracker. A GitHub account is needed to post. A parent or guardian should handle requests for a child.

Open public support tracker

Posts and attachments are public. Do not include learner names, history, PINs, Apple Account credentials, pairing codes or payment details. This is not a private support form.

Include the device model, system/app version, steps taken and exact error. Remove personal information from screenshots. Beta testers can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

Availability

The refreshed HartMath release is being prepared for the App Store. Existing beta testers can use the latest build offered in TestFlight. These guides describe the current practice, Pro and sharing features; older versions may need an update.
